# Legal Starter Copy Policy

This document defines the legal starter copy policy for Bazar Baz public surfaces.

## Pages

- `app/[locale]/privacy/page.tsx` — privacy notice
- `app/[locale]/terms/page.tsx` — terms of service
- `app/[locale]/trust/page.tsx` — trust and data ownership

## Source Module

`lib/content/b2b-legal-content.ts`

## Privacy Page Requirements

- Explain what kind of data may be processed.
- Cover business account data, customer-facing interaction data, contact/request-demo data, technical/security logs.
- Explain SMS/Web Push consent and configuration are controlled.
- State data is used to operate the platform and support businesses.
- State no public sale of customer lists.
- State businesses remain responsible for lawful use of their own customer data.
- State contact/request-demo data is used for follow-up.
- Include disclaimer: "این متن نسخه اولیه اطلاع‌رسانی حریم خصوصی است و قبل از استفاده رسمی تجاری باید توسط مشاور حقوقی بررسی شود."

## Terms Page Requirements

- Explain Bazar Baz is a business service platform.
- Accounts are for businesses/authorized users.
- Businesses are responsible for their own content/products/services/customer communications.
- Prohibited use: spam, illegal products/services, misuse of customer data, fake identities, abusive messaging.
- SMS/payment/notification features require configuration and may have provider costs/rules.
- Demo pages are examples.
- Dashboard access must be protected.
- Platform may change features/plans.
- Include disclaimer: "این متن پیش‌نویس شرایط استفاده است و برای انتشار نهایی باید از نظر حقوقی بررسی و تأیید شود."

## Trust Page Requirements

- B2B positioning clear.
- Data ownership emphasized.
- Dashboard safety explained.
- Demo-only examples emphasized.
- No unsupported security certification claims.
- No legal compliance guarantees.
- Include transparency disclaimer.

## Locale Support

- `fa` — full content
- `en` — full English copy
- `ar` — full Arabic copy

## Safety Rules

- Do not claim final legal approval.
- Do not claim official security certification unless it exists.
- Use drafting language and disclaimers.
- Do not create binding legal obligations through these pages.
