## Phase-one copy and behavior gaps

Candidate implementation update: the public planner now defaults to
coordination-only, validates dates/ages locally and navigates to contact with
the selection. It does not enter the retained future availability/hold path.
The original mismatch below records the finding that triggered this fix,
not the current default public behavior. No operational gate was enabled.

1. **Stay coordination wording conflicts with the booking module.** `content.js` and the localized policy text say date/guest selections only support coordination and do not reserve or pay. However, `booking.js` has Persian copy “انتخاب تاریخ برای بررسی موجودی و رزرو موقت” and a “check accommodation options” action, then contains availability and hold requests. This cannot be treated as a copy-only cleanup: the phase-one boundary requires the user-facing entry point and reachable behavior to stay coordination-only. Resolve before approving phase-one copy.
2. **The approved reference is not a content source.** Text, logos, apparent rooms and property imagery inside the desktop/mobile reference images cannot substantiate claims. Keep concept labels until approved originals and captions arrive.
3. **The questionnaire is not an approval record for every rendered claim.** Existing docs flag uncertain readings and say room counts/capacities, exact address, rates and policies were unresolved. The PDF and rewrite images are local-only inputs; preserve or copy approved extracts into project-managed content after the owner resolves discrepancies.
4. **Translation coverage exists, but approval is unproven.** English and Arabic pages are present. No evidence was found that a hotel owner/manager reviewed those translations or the transliteration of factual names. Do not translate ambiguous facts more confidently than Persian.
5. **VTour is a stated service, not an available website destination.** Keep the route as “display in preparation” unless a canonical destination and permission are provided. Phase one does not include player integration.
