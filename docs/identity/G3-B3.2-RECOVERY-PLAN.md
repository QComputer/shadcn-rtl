# G3-B3.2 — Hotel guest recovery without the original browser

Status: implementation plan, not authorization to activate or migrate Production. G3-B3.1b remains dependent on PR193. The existing reservation proof is tied to the original browser, tenant, property, reservation, origin and contact; expiration or loss currently fails closed. Customer phone identity and record-bound OTP establish control of a phone/session, not ownership. The reception directory is property-scoped search, and individual guest profiles are explicit staff-linked dossiers; neither proves that a current claimant owns a historical booking. Reception audit and waitlist records likewise are not ownership grants. The existing account password-recovery endpoint manages account credentials and sessions under its own availability gate; it is not reservation proof and must not be used to attach a stay.

## Proposed independently verifiable evidence

For future eligible bookings, issue a separate high-entropy, record-bound recovery secret at booking time, only after the customer has explicitly chosen to retain it. Store a domain-separated HMAC and expiry, never the raw secret. The customer must present that secret, a verified customer session, matching verified contact, explicit consent, and a new purpose-bound OTP for the exact reservation. A reference identifies a candidate but does not authorize it. The recovery secret must be provisioned and retained before the browser is lost; its delivery and safe storage UX need a separate threat review. A code shown only once can be lost and is not proof that the hotel sent or the guest received any other message.

Use the existing scoped context, customer session, quota/attempt controls and transactional OTP machinery. On redemption, validate tenant, property, verified origin, completed stay, active unexpired proof, customer/session/contact and unused secret in the same serializable transaction; atomically consume the recovery secret and OTP, create the ownership relation and sanitized audit. Bind every challenge to the record, user, session, tenant, origin and recovery-secret digest. Fail closed on ambiguity, duplicate ownership, concurrent redemption, account restrictions or mismatched proof. No financial, companion, reception-profile or booking snapshot changes. Default OFF and separately gated; no provider activity until independently authorized.

## Existing bookings and exceptional cases

PR193 did not issue independent recovery secrets. Do not derive one from a phone, reference, dossier, account, guest name or staff assertion, and do not backfill one from those values. Existing stays without such independently verifiable evidence remain unclaimable through automated recovery. A reception-assisted case may collect a customer request and audit a decision, but it must not grant ownership solely on a staff assertion; any additional evidence, retention policy and human workflow require explicit architecture and owner review before implementation. No phone-only, reference-only, knowledge-only or staff-only bypass.
