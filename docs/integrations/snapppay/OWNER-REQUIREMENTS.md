## P0 — Installment-only payment
- Reuse existing tenant-aware Retail payment/quote/order/payment-attempt infrastructure. Aka Shoes first tenant, provider disabled by default, tenant-specific readiness and credentials. Do not share credentials with other stores. Stage and Production separate. Keep ordinary CASH, BANK_TRANSFER and iNoti behavior intact, including guest checkout. SnappPay must not require a Bazarbaaz account; provider's own eligibility/auth rules still apply.
- **11% uplift for installment product prices only**, never a global price mutation or a fee added twice. Proposed deterministic policy: use the existing authorized ordinary merchandise price after applicable ordinary product/cart discounts; resolve allocation via existing discount engine, then compute +11% on net merchandise (not shipping or independent service charges), with explicit tax treatment agreed against existing rules and provider contract. Preserve normal cash/transfer price, discount semantics, currency and tax policy. Do not stack discounts in an inconsistent way. If discounts cannot be represented exactly in the provider cart schema, fail closed for SnappPay until resolved. Do not silently switch basis to total including freight/tax.
- Use integer minor units (IRR at provider boundary), no binary float. Proposed rounding: round half-up to nearest supported smallest unit at line level (or existing authoritative monetary rounding if compatible), deterministic allocation to unit amounts / discounts to reconcile provider cart. If schema requires indivisible per-unit amounts, use approved representable allocations or deny quote rather than send mismatched sums. Validate boundary and overflow. Example: 1,000,000 TOMAN normal merchandise => 1,110,000 TOMAN installment merchandise => 11,100,000 IRR, before other charges. Proposed rounding, tax and discount allocation require confirmation before live use.
- Product and checkout UI clearly distinguish normal and installment totals, show 11% increase before commitment, no hidden fees, no price surprise. Show actual eligible installment total; never promise installment schedule/credit approval not returned by provider. Requote when payment method/cart/discount changes; preserve immutable method-bound accepted quote, order line snapshot and attempt. Switching methods removes the uplift; retries/callbacks cannot recalculate it or apply it again. Provider amount, cart sum, stored amount, review UI and settlement reconcile exactly. Do not trust client monetary inputs. Preserve receipts/accounting clarity and merchant dashboard display. No invented commission/refund fields.
- Pricing approval is an explicit activation gate: 11% is owner-requested, not a verified SnappPay fee or permission to charge different prices. Obtain written provider/merchant contract confirmation and applicable legal/commercial review of installment uplift and disclosure. If prohibited, do not disguise it, move it to other fees, or enable payment; report alternative policy for owner decision. Mock/local development may continue disabled.
- Use observed INSTALLMENT value only if supported by Self contract. No other SnappPay payment type. No real provider financial calls, credential rotation, form submission or config changes without separate owner authorization. Verify official Self spec, sandbox, callback, static egress IP and merchant acceptance before live.
- Test: ordinary vs installment price, one/multiple quantities, discounts, shipping/tax, zero/edge amounts, rounding, method switching, quote tampering, stale quotes, duplicate attempts/callbacks, timeout/unknown outcome, wrong tenant, wrong amount, guest and authenticated checkout, PWA redirect/return, receipts, cancellation/reconciliation (only documented operations). Use isolated PG + real local HTTP/browser and deterministic provider mocks. No source-text-only substitutes.
